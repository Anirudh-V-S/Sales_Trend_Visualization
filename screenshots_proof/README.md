# 📂 Screenshots Proof

This directory contains clear, high-resolution `.png` screenshots capturing the live, working dashboard of our **Sales Trend Visualization** project as proof of dynamic functionality and rigorous end-to-end QA.

## 📸 Captured Proof Interfaces
The screenshots demonstrate:
1.  **Overview Dashboard:** Interactive KPI metric tiles, executive summaries, and dual-axis volume comparison charts.
2.  **Date & Category Filters:** Live sidebar filter controls updating the central charts.
3.  **Temporal Trend Module:** Daily, weekly, monthly, and quarterly time granularities with regression trendline fits.
4.  **Multi-Dimensional Revenue:** Revenue pie shares, ranks, and dynamic cross-tabulations.
5.  **Product Analytics:** Top-10 and bottom-5 horizontal bars, nested Treemaps, and product searches.
6.  **Category Standings:** Ranks, standings indicators, and revenue details.
7.  **Period-over-Period Growth:** Color-coded growth rates and numeric correlation matrixes.
8.  **Automated Business Insights:** 80/20 rule validation and strategic corporate recommendations.
9.  **Data Preprocessing Pipeline:** Successful execution of duplicate cleaning and missing values imputation.
